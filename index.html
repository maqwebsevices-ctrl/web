GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
We've got answers.

Here's what businesses often ask before starting a new website.

Ask us something else
How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on how many pages you need, the design style and any special features. Share your requirements and we can discuss a package that fits your budget.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive design is at the heart of every package, so layouts adapt to smaller phones, tablets and larger screens.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

We build with SEO foundations such as clear headings, descriptive page metadata, accessible markup and search-friendly structure. Strong Google rankings require ongoing content, competition analysis and optimisation and cannot be guaranteed.

Can I ask for extra pages or features?

Absolutely. Tell us if you need a gallery, menu, enquiry form, booking option or something else. We'll discuss the scope and a suitable quote before building it.

Do domain and hosting come included?

Domain registration, hosting and third-party services depend on your project and are clarified in your quote. This keeps costs transparent.
